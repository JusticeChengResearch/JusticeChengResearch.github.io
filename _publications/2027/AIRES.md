abstract: >-
  This study develops and validates the AI Reliance in Education Scale (AIRES), a psychometric instrument grounded in scaffolding theory that conceptualizes AI reliance as a developmental trajectory rather than an addiction-like symptom. An item pool refined through pilot testing and cognitive interviews yielded 31 items administered to 617 students, randomly split into independent exploratory and confirmatory subsamples. A four-factor, 12-item structure emerged and achieved good fit (CFI = .965, TLI = .952, RMSEA = .061, SRMR = .057). To rule out sample-specific artifacts, the model was re-estimated across seven additional random splits, with fit remaining stable throughout. Results position AIRES as a reliable and valid measure of students’ AI reliance in higher education.
